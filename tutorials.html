BEGINNER

How to build a strong short-form edit

Start with a clear hook, remove dead space, keep the story moving and use text only when it improves understanding. A strong edit makes every second earn its place.

Simple workflow

  1. Choose one clear idea.
  2. Put the strongest moment near the beginning.
  3. Cut pauses and repetition.
  4. Add readable text and purposeful sound.
  5. Watch the final edit without touching the timeline and note anything that feels slow.
TECHNIQUE

Transitions that feel natural

Transitions work best when they support the movement or meaning of a scene. Match movement, direction, framing or sound instead of adding an effect just because it looks impressive.

Try this

End one clip with movement in one direction and begin the next clip with related movement. Add a short sound cue only if it improves the transition.

STORYTELLING

Make captions easier to watch

Use short phrases, strong contrast and enough screen time for viewers to read comfortably. Keep captions away from important faces and interface controls.

Rule of thumb

One idea per caption beat. If a sentence is long, split it into natural phrases rather than shrinking the text.
